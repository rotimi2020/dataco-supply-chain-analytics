# DataCo Supply Chain Analytics Project

## WF01: Data Preparation

**Purpose:** Clean, standardize, and prepare the raw DataCo supply chain dataset for reliable analysis.

**Objective:** Produce a consistent, analysis-ready dataset by handling data types, standardizing column names, converting date fields, filtering unnecessary columns, and exporting the prepared data.

## WF02: Descriptive Analytics — 10 Business Questions

### Question 1: Overall Delivery Performance
What percentage of order items are delivered late, on time, shipped in advance, or cancelled?

### Question 2: Shipping Mode Performance
Which shipping modes have the highest number of late deliveries, and how do their late-delivery rates compare?

### Question 3: Product Category Profitability
Which product categories generate the highest sales revenue and recorded profit, and which categories perform poorly?

### Question 4: Customer Segment Performance
Which customer segments generate the highest sales revenue?

### Question 5: Regional Sales Performance
Which order regions generate the highest and lowest sales revenue?

### Question 6: Late Delivery Rate by Shipping Mode
Which shipping mode has the highest late-delivery rate after accounting for the number of order items handled by each mode?

### Question 7: Department Performance
Which departments contribute the most to total sales revenue?

### Question 8: Market Performance
Which markets generate the highest and lowest sales revenue?

### Question 9: Actual vs. Scheduled Shipping Time
How do actual shipping times compare with scheduled shipping times, and how often do deliveries take longer than expected?

### Question 10: Monthly Sales Trend
How does sales revenue change from month to month, and are there noticeable increases, decreases, or seasonal patterns?

## WF03: Late Delivery Prediction

**Purpose:** Build and evaluate a machine-learning model to predict late-delivery risk using available order, product, customer, and shipping features.

## WF04: Order Volume Forecasting

**Purpose:** Forecast monthly unique order volume for the next 12 months using historical order data and ARIMA time-series models.
